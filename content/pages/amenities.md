## Amenities & Features

Argyle Place is designed for comfortable, practical city living with amenities residents rely on every day.

### In Your Apartment
- Island or separate kitchen with oven/range and refrigerator
- Ceramic tile bathrooms
- 3–4 closets per unit plus extra storage options
- Plush carpet or carpet/tile mix flooring
- Ceiling fans and window coverings
- Secure intercom/entry buzzer

### Building Amenities
- 2 elevators in a vintage mid-rise building
- 24-hour on-site laundry room
- Bike storage room and storage closets
- On-site management and maintenance team
- Controlled access entry
- Parking available (surface lot and street)

### Included in Rent
- Heat and cooking gas
- Water
- High-speed internet and cable

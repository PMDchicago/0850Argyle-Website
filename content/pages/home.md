## Welcome to Argyle Place

Argyle Place offers the best of city living in Chicago's vibrant Uptown neighborhood. With 88 well-maintained apartments, convenient transit access, and a walkable location near Lake Michigan, this is urban living at its finest.

- 88 total units across studio, 1-bedroom, and 2-bedroom layouts

- Steps from Foster Beach, Andersonville shops, and Asia on Argyle dining

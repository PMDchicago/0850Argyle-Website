## The Neighborhood

Located at **840–850 W. Argyle St., Chicago, IL 60640**, Argyle Place sits at the crossroads of Uptown and Andersonville — two of Chicago's most vibrant and diverse neighborhoods.

### Transit
- 2 blocks to the **Argyle Red Line** station (20 minutes to the Loop)
- Steps from CTA express bus stops on Broadway, Marine Drive, and Sheridan Road
- ¼ mile from Lake Shore Drive for easy highway access

### Lakefront & Parks
- 2 blocks to **Foster Beach** and the Lakefront Trail
- Near **Margate Park** and other green spaces along the lake

### Dining & Shopping
- Walking distance to the **Asia on Argyle** corridor — one of Chicago's best food destinations
- Short walk to **Andersonville** on Clark Street for independent shops, bars, and restaurants
- Nearby **Mariano's** and neighborhood grocery markets

### Entertainment & Culture
- Close to the **Aragon Ballroom** and **Riviera Theatre**
- Rich nightlife and cultural scene in Uptown
- Community centers, libraries, and local services nearby
